## Biography
Alice Vance is an experienced People Operations leader who pioneered OCI's unified workforce management model treating human staff and feline employees with equal structural importance. With over a decade of experience in corporate HR and animal sanctuary management, Alice ensures that every new hire receives comprehensive onboarding checklist tracking—from laptop provisioning for humans to preferred perch allocation for cats. She works closely with Forgejo issue workflows and Frappe HR to maintain seamless workplace operations and employee well-being.
